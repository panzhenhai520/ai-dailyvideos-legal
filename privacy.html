Privacy Policy — AI DailyVideos

Effective date: October 10, 2026
Contact: thonpany@gmail.com

1. Data We Handle

AI DailyVideos is a personal tool with a single user (its owner). To publish videos, it handles:

  • TikTok OAuth tokens (access token and refresh token), stored securely on the owner's private server and used solely to publish videos to the owner's own TikTok account;
  • Video files being published;
  • Basic account information (open_id and username) returned by TikTok Login Kit to identify the connected account.

2. How We Use It

This data is used solely to operate the publishing tool: authenticating with TikTok and posting the owner's videos. Nothing else.

3. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We do not share data with third parties, except with TikTok's official APIs as required to publish content.

4. Retention

OAuth tokens are retained until the owner revokes access. Video files are retained for operational purposes.
