AI DailyVideos

A personal automation tool that produces short finance-education videos and publishes them to the owner's own TikTok account — through TikTok's official Content Posting API only.
Terms of Service Privacy Policy OAuth Callback

What it does

AI DailyVideos turns one finance topic a day into a short explainer video (inflation, dollar-cost averaging, saving systems — in Chinese, for a global Chinese-speaking audience). A content pipeline researches the topic, drafts the script, synthesizes the voiceover, generates the visuals and assembles the MP4. This tool handles only the last mile: publishing.

How publishing works

  1. Connect once — the owner authorizes via TikTok Login Kit (OAuth 2.0 + PKCE). No TikTok passwords are ever handled.
  2. Direct Post — finished videos are published straight to the owner's profile via the Content Posting API.
  3. Draft upload — alternatively a video can be sent to the creator's inbox as a draft, and the owner finishes publishing inside the TikTok app.
  4. AI disclosure — every video published through this tool is labeled as AI-generated, in line with TikTok's AI-content disclosure requirements.

Principles

  • Official APIs only — no scraping, no credential handling, no unofficial clients.
  • Single user — the tool publishes to the owner's own account, nothing else.
  • Human in the loop — scripts are reviewed before production; draft mode keeps final publishing inside the TikTok app.
